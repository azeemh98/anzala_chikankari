Our Story

200 years of needlework, one thread at a time.

Founded in 2016, ANJALA was born from a deep love for the hand-embroidery traditions of Lucknow. Chikankari began in the courts of Lucknow over 200 years ago and is still worked entirely by hand today — every kurta, saree and personalized name frame in our collection passes through an artisan's hands for 3 to 10 days, outlined in kachche taanka and filled with dozens of traditional stitches.

We work directly with over 40 artisan families and embroidery clusters around Lucknow, paying fairly for every hour of handwork and preserving authentic stitches like bakhiya (shadow work), phanda, jaali and murri. No two pieces are ever quite identical — that's the beauty of handwork.

Whether you're shopping for an everyday kurta or a hand-embroidered name frame for a nursery wall, our team is happy to guide you — book an appointment for a personal styling session, or shop the full collection online with worldwide shipping.
