Our Craft

200 years of needlework, one thread at a time.

Chikankari began in the courts of Lucknow and is still worked entirely by hand today. Each ANJALA piece passes through an artisan's hands for 3 to 10 days — outlined in kachche taanka, filled with dozens of traditional stitches, then washed and pressed by hand.

We work directly with embroidery clusters around Lucknow, paying artisans fairly for every hour of handwork that goes into your order.
